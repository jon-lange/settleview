# Learnings ledger

The single place for what this project has learned: every hypothesis tested, its verdict and the evidence, plus hard-won facts about Kalshi's API and our own system. Update it whenever a test finishes. Newest first within each section. Numbers are on held-back data unless stated.

**Testing standard (applies to every row):** entries only at prices that really traded (Kalshi trade prints or candle bid/ask), 1¢ slippage, exact Kalshi taker fee (0.07·C·P·(1−P), rounded up to $0.0001; makers pay 0), Kalshi's official result as the outcome, train on the first two-thirds of days and judge on the last third, window-clustered t, correction for the number of rules tested, criteria written down before results. Data: 2,848 KXBTC15M windows, 2026-09-29 → 2026-10-28 (`data/research/kalshi_trades.db`, `data/btcflow.db:index_1s`).

## Facts about the market and our model

- **Kalshi's 15m BTC prices are well calibrated** in every price bucket; strategies that buy at a price lose roughly the fee. Win rate is meaningless without the price paid (a 90% hit rate on 90¢ contracts loses).
- **Our Coinbase index vs Kalshi's settlement index (CF Benchmarks RTI 60s average):** median $1.82 below (open average also $1.81 below the strike, so the offset mostly cancels); 90% within $8.21; worst $24.05. Proxy outcome disagrees with Kalshi's result in 58 of 2,804 windows (2.1%): 8% within $25 of the strike, 17.5% within $10, 28.3% within $5 — all mismatches within $25.
- **Our σ vs market-implied σ:** ~1.03× at 10–12 min, 1.11–1.25× in the last 6 min (highest 12:00–16:00 UTC and in volatile periods). Too-high σ near the close pushes our odds toward 50% and makes long shots look cheap — the root of the early long-shot losses.
- **The Coach's earlier "calibrated" claim was vs a coin flip, not vs the market.** Always benchmark against the market price.
- **Setups on honest prices are rare** (1–5/hour on the old rule; fewer with the longshot floor and 50/50 market blend).
- **External evidence (survey):** takers lose ~1.3%/trade in Kalshi crypto, makers earn ~1.3% (Bürgi, Deng & Whelan 2026); Polymarket 15m takers won 53% of fills and lost money, makers won 47% and made money; contracts under ~20–25¢ win less than their price; a careful public backtest of a model like ours lost to the market (t −3.43).
- **Agent-mode scope (2026-10-30):** H-LAG (Kalshi stale after a ≥0.15%/60 s external BTC move) was not tested for the agent: an LLM-in-the-loop wake takes 10–30 s, longer than the lag such an edge lives in, and Binance is geo-blocked here; H-SPREAD (resting two-sided opening bids) is out of scope because the app sends only immediate-or-cancel orders.
- **X/Twitter sentiment (Grok summary shared by the user, 2026-10-29):** consensus is that simple KXBTC15M rules (late favourite, high win rate, mean reversion) lose to calibration plus the taker fee; one user ran 53 strategies live and none cleared their error bars. Claimed exceptions: 'panic fade' (fade sharp short-term BTC moves) in April-2026 Turbine backtests, not confirmed later; final-minute favourite buying modelled on the in-flight 60 s average (~+1.9c after fees in one sample) = our final_minute lab mode. Untested here: BTC-move panic fade (our H9 faded a 10c contract move: KILLED) and 'skip the next window after a loss'. Follow-up (same day): KalshiSpy shows verified accounts net positive on KXBTC15M (>$1.3M across tracked winning positions) but not how; no public evidence that simple rules do it. One more untested lead: Coinbase 1-minute velocity leading Kalshi's price (a May-2026 study).

- **S6 liquidation rider (OPEN, untested):** ride >$500k one-sided 2-min perp liquidations on 5m/15m markets (YouTube bot, no results shown, live loss on camera). Needs an OKX/Hyperliquid liquidation recorder (Binance/Bybit geo-blocked). See polymarket-short-term-strategies.md S6.

## Polymarket US API facts (paid for in production)

- **The markets WebSocket allows 10 subscriptions per connection** (undocumented; observed 2026-10-29 when 15m + 5m
  windows needed 12: "max subscriptions per connection reached", replayed exactly at limit 10). The old single-socket
  stream also kept refused request ids as if live, so a refused market read as subscribed and `trades_since` said "no
  trades" instead of "unknown". Fixed with a connection pool (3 markets x 3 types each) and refusal detection.
- **The public REST BBO (`/v1/markets/{slug}/bbo`) is a cached snapshot** (changed on ~3% of 1-second samples): never
  price an order or compare venues from it. Use the authenticated markets WebSocket (MARKET_DATA_LITE, ~9 msg/s).
  A penny-test IOC buy at the REST ask did not fill; at the WebSocket ask + 1c it filled at once (2026-10-29).
- **Positions come back as a dict keyed by market slug** (`netPosition`, `cost`, `avgPx`); there is no slug field inside.
- **The positions endpoint lags fills by a second or more** (gateway penny test 2026-10-29): right after an IoC sale it
  still showed +1; right after a Down buy it showed nothing, so a sell capped by that read was skipped. Held quantity
  must come from our own fill answers / the private stream; REST positions are a backup only.
- **Down = BUY_SHORT / SELL_SHORT works** (1 contract each way filled); its execution prices come back in NO terms
  (bought at 66c with YES 34/35). Cancel answer and get_order-after-cancel agreed (`canceled`).
- **A post-only (participateDontInitiate) buy at ask+2c rested instead of being rejected** — semantics unclear.
- Fees: 1 contract at 33-68c cost $0.02 each (0.0695·P·(1−P) ≈ 1.5-1.7c, rounded to the nearest cent).
- **Fees: rounded to the NEAREST cent (half up) per order, not up:** 2 contracts at 22c (raw $0.0239) and at 17c (raw
  $0.0196) each cost $0.02; rounding up would have charged $0.03 for the 22c pair. The 1-lots above fit either rule.
  Corollary: a 1-lot at 95-99c (raw < 0.5c) pays no fee. Code: `polymarket_fee`, `VenueSpec.fee_rounding = "nearest_cent"`
  (wording corrected 2026-11-01; earlier docs said "rounded up").
- **Settlement equals Kalshi KXBTC15M to the cent** (same CF Benchmarks BRTI 60 s average; 3,293/3,293 results since
  2026-09-24). History prices are 1-minute display snapshots ~1 min late: no fair backtests on them.

## Kalshi API and system facts (paid for in production)

- **Never use GET /markets (list) or /markets/{t} for live prices** — they lag the order book by a median of ~15 s (list) and up to 11 s (single). Use the WebSocket `orderbook_delta` (seq-checked) or GET /markets/{t}/orderbook.
- **GET /portfolio/orders/{id} returns stale "resting" or 404 for 20+ s after a cancel in production** (demo is consistent). A cancel response is authoritative; read the order list by client_order_id. On demo, the per-market list briefly misses a new order — fall back to the account-wide list.
- **A raw retry of an amend can 404 while the order is still resting** (demo); resolve unknown outcomes through the order list.
- **reduce_only is IoC-only** ("reduce_only can only be used with IoC orders", prod penny test). The resting take-profit therefore cannot be reduce-only; the stop cancels it before its reduce-only IOC sale.
- **Taker fee rounds UP to $0.0001 per fill** (14/14 production fills); maker fee 0 on KXBTC15M fills so far. A GitHub claim of whole-cent rounding is outdated.
- **Crypto markets live on exchange shard 2.** Orders don't move funds between shards; `POST /portfolio/intra_exchange_instance_transfer` and `target_balance_allocation` exist. Order groups must be created on shard 2 or shard-2 orders are rejected (`order_group_not_found`).
- **Rate limits (Basic tier):** ~100 write tokens/s (create/amend/decrease 10, cancel 2 per order), 200 read tokens/s; public bulk downloads throttle at ~4 req/s with 429s.
- **No comments/chat in the API** (the "communications" endpoints are RFQs).
- **Our per-second index row lands ~1–2 s after its second ends** (`aggregator.py` lateness), so finalized rows are 2.7–3.6 s old — too stale for final-minute and maker decisions; they need a live price tap.
- **Stops:** 1-second polling saw the bid 4¢ past the stop and, with a stale-GET bug, stalled 20+ s; on the WebSocket path a stop filled exactly at its trigger with the cancel+sale in ~70 ms.

- Phone Play (strategy "you", 2026-10-29): first real round trip from the phone over the LAN worked end to end — a Kalshi entry at 54c, EXIT NOW at 83c two minutes later (P&L from fills). Found in prod: the bots' paper-setup gate blocked the user's own calls (removed for you/you_poly; phone limits $50/trade, $250/day instead) and the fixed buy bar covered the target tiles. Two-tap confirm felt slow ("timing is key") -> replaced by one slide.

- Phone Play stops (2026-10-29 15:16 Kalshi, 15:50 Polymarket): bot-style default BTC stops closed two of the user's own trades within 20 s on ordinary noise (DOWN 58c stopped at 44c: stop $2 from BTC; DOWN 61c stopped at 48c: BTC +$47 in 18 s through a $25-room stop). A binary contract's loss is capped at the stake, and our studies found stops cost about what they save, so the phone now has NO default stop: the stop-loss is opt-in after entry (checkbox + distance), drawn on the chart.

## Kalshi perps (margin) API facts

From `scripts/quick/perps_demo_checks.py reads` (demo, read-only, 2026-10-30) and Kalshi's public production market
reads; spec `perps_openapi.yaml` / `perps_asyncapi.yaml` from docs.kalshi.com. Evidence:
`data/research/perps_demo_checks/reads_20261030T075935Z.json`.

- **Perps have their own hosts:** REST `external-api.kalshi.com` / demo `external-api.demo.kalshi.co` (`/trade-api/v2/margin/...`),
  WebSocket `external-api-margin-ws.kalshi.com` / demo `external-api-margin-ws.demo.kalshi.co`, path
  `/trade-api/ws/v2/margin`. The handshake signs `GET /trade-api/ws/v2/margin` like REST (demo: opened in ~400 ms).
- **The BTC ticker differs by environment:** production `KXBTCPERP`, demo `KXBTCPERP1`.
- **Whole contracts only:** `fractional_trading_enabled` is false for BTC in both environments (the spec allows 0.01
  steps in general). One contract = 0.0001 BTC; tick $0.0001 per contract; price about $7.80 at $78k.
- **Leverage estimates (both envs, 2026-10-30):** ~6.85x long, ~6.38x short at $1k notional, slightly lower at larger
  sizes. Initial margin = maintenance x 1.3. Liquidation margin ratio: production 1.0 (queue entry 0.91), demo 0.97 (0.8).
- **Fees (demo, BTC):** taker 0.12% of notional, maker 0.02%. Funding cap 2% per interval, deadband 0.01%; next funding
  12:00 UTC (8-hour intervals). The demo's funding estimate was 1.5% for one interval: the demo book is thin
  (bid 7.40/ask 8.03 levels) and its funding is not representative.
- **Positions in Kalshi's own app are readable:** `GET /margin/positions` (all subaccounts when `subaccount` is omitted)
  and `/margin/balance` tag each entry with `subaccount_type` (`cross` / `isolated` / `auto_rebalance`). No isolated
  write path is needed to show them (R17 stands as written).
- **Subaccount transfer** (`POST /portfolio/margin/subaccounts/transfer`, body `client_transfer_id` UUID,
  `from_subaccount`, `to_subaccount`, `amount_cents`) is documented as synchronous ("Transfer completed successfully",
  empty body); the event-contract `intra_exchange_instance_transfer` is the asynchronous one. Not yet exercised.
- **There is no "list subaccounts" endpoint;** `/margin/balance` lists every subaccount's balance, which is how a
  configured subaccount number is validated.
- **The demo account is NOT margin-enabled** (`GET /margin/enabled` false; balance, risk and exit-trigger reads return
  403 "user is not enabled for margin trading"). Public and order/fill list reads work. Demo order checks (U1 step 2)
  cannot run until margin is enabled on the demo account.
- `GET /margin/funding_history` requires `start_date`.
- **Production account reads (2026-10-30, read-only, `scripts/quick/perps_prod_checks.py reads`):** margin enabled;
  a small test balance on the primary (subaccount 0); no numbered subaccounts (1-63) exist; a subaccount
  **64** with $0 is listed (apparently the slot for Kalshi-app isolated positions). **Production omits
  `subaccount_type`** from balance and positions, although the spec marks it required: app-held positions must be told
  apart by subaccount number. BTC notional risk limit $500; taker 0.12%, maker 0.02%; API usage tier "basic"
  (read 400/s, write 100/s tokens).
- **Kalshi's own app sends far protective prices:** a round trip in Kalshi's app at 04:27 UTC was a 9-contract bid capped
  at 8.4423 and an ask floored at 7.1911 with BTC near $7.80 per contract (about 8% through the market, both filled).
- **Creating a numbered perps subaccount needs the "advanced" API usage level:** `POST /portfolio/margin/subaccounts`
  returned 403 `subaccount_creation_requires_advanced_api_usage_level` on the basic tier (production, 2026-10-30; the
  first attempt without a JSON body returned 400 `invalid_content_type`). Nothing was created or moved. **The user
  then changed KD4: Settleview trades perps from the primary perps account (subaccount 0), with "your whole perps
  balance is at risk" wording; no transfers.**
- **Real-money check on the primary perps account (2026-10-30 08:13 UTC, user's go-ahead, `perps_prod_checks.py
  orders --subaccount 0`; evidence `data/research/perps_prod_checks/orders_20261030T081408Z.json`,
  `tests/fixtures/kalshi_margin_ws/prod_private_capture.json`):** 4 fills of 1 contract, flat at the end; the
  cost was four $0.0094 fees (P&L $0.0000).
  - **IOC 0.5% through the touch is accepted** (bid capped at 7.8360 with ask 7.7971 filled at 7.7970); create
    answers in ~30 ms with `fill_count`, `average_fill_price`, `average_fee_paid`. Price banding did not refuse it.
  - **Fee = 0.12% of notional rounded UP to $0.0001** (7.797 x 0.0012 = 0.009356 -> 0.0094), every fill.
  - **Duplicate `client_order_id` -> 409 `order_already_exists`**; a 23-character id is accepted.
  - **Stop past liquidation -> 400 `stop_loss_past_liquidation`.**
  - **A trigger anchored to a finished IOC order is ACCEPTED** (status active, count 1.00), not refused; the plan
    expected 409. Irrelevant to the design: whole-position triggers are used (KTD6).
  - **Whole-position bracket:** `count` "0.00"; an edit keeps the same id and changes `updated_time` (KTD9 binds it);
    it covered a later add; once the position is flat the trigger is gone from GET (empty list).
  - **A fired leg sends a `system` reduce-only IOC** with client id `etr:<trigger_id>:0`, priced ~1.5% through the
    mark (7.6803 vs mark 7.797; self-trade prevention `maker`), filling at the touch. A tight bracket (mark +/- 2 ticks)
    fired in ~5 s on the take-profit side. The WebSocket fill carries `client_order_id`, `fee_cost`, `post_position`,
    `subaccount`, `order_source` (no `order_reason`).
  - **`GET /margin/positions` lags fills by one step** (showed 1 after the add to 2, then 2 after the reduce to 1):
    trust fills / `post_position` first, REST positions as the backup (like Polymarket).
  - **Liquidation calibration (1 contract long at 7.7970 on the small test equity):** Kalshi's `/margin/risk` gave a
    liquidation price, maintenance $0.8752 (11.22% of notional) and position leverage 8.909. **Kalshi's `leverage_estimate` is
    1 / INITIAL margin rate**: 6.8473 (long) x 1.3 = 8.90. So maintenance rate = 1 / (estimate x
    initial_margin_multiplier), and the most an entry can open at is the estimate itself. With that rate the KTD7
    formula long P = (qE - C) / (q (1 - mmr/theta)) matches Kalshi's liquidation price within 0.01%. The account's
    `initial_margin` read 1.0808 (1.235 x maintenance), not 1.3x: unexplained, noted.

## Process lessons

- **Postgres CI failures that SQLite passes (2026-11-01):** (1) the perps View "under 5 ms" was a real hot-path cost. It ran 4 selects per call (one `trading_brackets` read per event-contract env). That was 0.65 ms on SQLite and 2.7 ms on local Postgres, and 5.8 ms on CI. It is now 2 selects (about 2.1 ms on local Postgres), with the same figures, still read fresh. (2) A "book feed broken" test that served a quote was a test race: it waited on a condition that the capture's 12th of 57 messages already met. The freshness rule itself was correct. docs/solutions/2026-11-01-perps-view-round-trips-on-postgres.md, docs/solutions/2026-11-01-wait-for-the-message-under-test.md.
- **Internet outage at the host = unprotected positions** (2026-10-29 10:26–10:29 CT, DNS failures): every feed dropped; BTC crossed a live Polymarket PNW stop at 10:29:19 while offline, the stop correctly refused to act on a stale index, and the $1 trade settled at −$0.70 instead of roughly −$0.25. Feeds reconnected on their own. Same root cause class as the overnight reboot: all protection lives on one machine (an always-on host would remove both).
- **Same signal, different fills across venues:** at 10:22:49 PNW fired on both; Kalshi's ask moved 65→67c before the order and the 1c entry slack refused it, Polymarket filled at 68c. Track fill-vs-refusal per venue.

- **The app is a single point of failure for stops.** A Windows Update reboot killed the app at 01:41 CDT with live trade 8 open: its resting take-profit (on Kalshi) still filled at 99¢, but the index stop lives in the app and was unwatched. Any unattended live run needs auto-restart (service/scheduled task) and Windows Update active hours/pause; the second overnight session never started.

- Small live penny tests find real bugs that sandbox/demo cannot (two production-only bugs found for ~70¢ total on 2026-10-28).
- Shadow (real order book, simulated fills) is the right testing/training ground; Kalshi demo is a separate thin market with different behaviour.
- A paper record built on stale quotes looks profitable and isn't — the "edge" came from acting on prices already gone.
- Overfitting is the main risk of rapid testing: count every variant, hold out data, re-check survivors on new data (Shadow lab) before pennies.
