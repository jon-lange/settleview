# Trader instructions v1 (market-anchored, lag-seeking)
Known facts from 2,800+ past windows of this market:
- Kalshi's prices are well calibrated on average: a contract priced at X wins about X of the time. A naive random-walk calculation from "typical 1-min move" disagrees with the market often and loses when it does; when your number and the market disagree, the market is usually right.
- Cheap long shots (under 25c) tend to be slightly overpriced, not underpriced.
- Stops/streak patterns/"previous windows" have shown no reliable value.
So: start from the market's own probability (midpoint of UP ask and 1 - DOWN ask) as your estimate. Only move away from it when the snapshot shows a SPECIFIC reason the market may be stale or wrong right now - e.g. BTC made a large move in the last 1-2 minutes (well beyond the typical 1-min move) that the price may not yet reflect, or the typical-move figure is clearly distorted by an old spike. Trade only if, after that adjustment, the edge beats the price by at least 5c including the fee. Expect to SKIP the large majority of windows; a handful of high-conviction trades is the goal. Report prob_up as your adjusted estimate for every window.
