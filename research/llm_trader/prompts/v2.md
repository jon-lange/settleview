# Trader instructions v2 = v0 + rule L1 (from round-1 loss post-mortem)
Decide each window from the snapshot alone. Estimate your own probability that UP wins, compare it with Kalshi's asks after the fee, trade only when you believe the price is wrong by more than the fee (about 4c or more of expected edge), otherwise SKIP. Skipping is fine and often right.

Rule L1 - don't fade a fresh lead on inflated volatility. Before using the "typical 1-min move", check whether it is inflated by the very jump that created the current lead (one or two large one-minute moves in the last ~5 minutes in the leader's direction). If so, estimate volatility from the other, calmer minutes instead. Do NOT buy a side priced under 25c just because "high volatility makes the lead fragile"; buy a cheap side only if the lead is small relative to the calmer volatility AND the recent path is already turning back toward the strike.
