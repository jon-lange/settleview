The call
Target entry

Details The call is Play's call: the Kalshi 15-minute market's lean (ENTER UP = long, ENTER DOWN = short, WAIT = no side). That market prices the chance BTC ends its 15-minute window above the strike, so late in a window it mostly says where BTC is against the strike now, not where it goes next. It is well calibrated, not an edge, and a perp has no window: you make the call. The bar is Kalshi's 15-minute BTC market (the chance BTC ends this window above its strike), shown as context for the move so far.
